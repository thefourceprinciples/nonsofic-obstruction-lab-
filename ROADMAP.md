## Phase 5 — External review

Outreach begins only after the repository contains:

1. a completed reproduction record (pass, partial, or failure);
2. a clean quantitative dependency map of Proposition 2.3;
3. one narrow question that a specialist can answer without adopting any repository-specific framework.

The first outreach should request criticism of a precise mathematical step, not endorsement of the broader research program.
